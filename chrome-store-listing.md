# Chrome Web Store Listing

## Extension Name
Page Summary

## Short Description (132 characters max)
Instantly summarize any webpage. Extract key points, stats, and save summaries to your personal dashboard with Google sign-in.

## Detailed Description
Page Summary is a powerful productivity tool that helps you quickly understand any webpage's content.

**Key Features:**

- **One-Click Summaries** - Get an instant summary of any webpage with a single click
- **Key Points Extraction** - Automatically identifies and lists the most important points
- **Page Statistics** - See word count, link count, image count, and heading count at a glance
- **Personal Dashboard** - Save summaries to review later (requires sign-in)
- **Google Sign-In** - Secure authentication with your Google account
- **Cloud Sync** - Your saved summaries are stored securely and accessible anytime

**How It Works:**

1. Navigate to any webpage you want to summarize
2. Click the Page Summary extension icon
3. View the instant summary with key points and statistics
4. Optionally sign in to save summaries to your dashboard

**Perfect For:**

- Researchers gathering information from multiple sources
- Students studying online materials
- Professionals reviewing articles and reports
- Anyone who wants to save time reading long webpages

**Privacy:**
We respect your privacy. Page Summary only accesses the content of pages you actively choose to summarize. Your data is securely stored and never shared with third parties.
